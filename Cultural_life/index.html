When you click on a museum icon on the map, an interactive bubble will appear in that area. The size of each bubble reflects the number of nearby exhibitions and estimated visitor flow—larger bubbles indicate greater popularity. Bubble colors differentiate museum categories (Art, History, Science, etc.). Hover over any bubble to reveal the museum’s name and its total exhibition count at a glance.
